## 直接答案段

Gay intended parents preparing for or following MHB New York 2026 commonly need a clear way to compare provider responsibilities, screening and matching, egg donation and IVF coordination, costs and third-party services, and legal and parentage handoffs. Use the same five questions with each provider: What does the agency handle directly? How are screening and rematching explained? Who owns donor, embryo, and clinic decisions? Which costs are included or paid to independent providers? How are legal, insurance, escrow, communication, pregnancy, and post-birth responsibilities coordinated? Yunda provides education, coordination, bilingual communication, and case-management support; medical, legal, insurance, IVF, and escrow decisions belong to the relevant qualified professionals.

## 五个问题模块

### 1. What does the agency handle directly?

Ask the provider to separate agency coordination from clinic, legal, insurance, escrow, and other independent professional work. Record the named case owner, handoff points, communication frequency, and what happens when the plan changes.

### 2. How are screening, matching, and rematching explained?

Ask which information is collected before a match, which reviews belong to medical or mental-health professionals, how both sides discuss expectations, and how a rematch decision and related costs are handled. Screening and matching do not guarantee a match, pregnancy, or birth outcome.

### 3. Who owns donor, embryo, and IVF decisions?

Ask who explains donor options, screening records, embryo creation, clinic timing, laboratory coordination, transport, and medical recommendations. Keep clinic decisions with qualified fertility professionals and document how the agency communicates across the team.

### 4. Which costs are included, variable, or paid to third parties?

Request a written, like-for-like comparison covering agency services, donor and medical costs, legal work, insurance, escrow, travel, contingencies, refunds, and rematching. Do not compare headline totals that use different assumptions.

### 5. How do legal, parentage, insurance, escrow, and support handoffs work?

Ask when each party should retain independent counsel, who reviews insurance, who administers escrow, how parentage documents are coordinated, and how communication continues through pregnancy, delivery, and post-birth support.
