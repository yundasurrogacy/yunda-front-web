### 建议正文

#### What did we learn from MHB New York 2026?

MHB New York 2026 reinforced a practical lesson for intended parents: a productive conference conversation is the beginning of due diligence, not a substitute for a case plan. The most useful follow-up is to write down who handles each step, which decisions belong to independent medical, legal, insurance, IVF, or escrow professionals, and what information still needs verification. Families should compare providers using the same questions about screening, matching, rematching, donor and embryo coordination, communication, fees, and post-birth handoffs. Yunda’s MHB participation is documented through the event recap and approved event media. Yunda provides education, coordination, bilingual communication, and case-management support; it does not replace the qualified professionals responsible for medical, legal, insurance, IVF, or escrow decisions.

#### 1. Clarity is more useful than a broad promise

Ask a provider to separate its direct coordination role from the work performed by clinics, attorneys, insurance professionals, escrow administrators, and other independent specialists. A clear handoff map makes the next decision easier to verify.

#### 2. A comparison needs the same questions for every provider

Use one written checklist for screening, matching, rematching, donor and embryo planning, communication, fees, and support. Comparing headline totals or general promises without the same assumptions can create a misleading picture.

#### 3. Complex decisions need the right professional at the right time

An agency may coordinate communication, but medical recommendations, legal advice, insurance determinations, IVF decisions, and escrow administration belong to their respective qualified professionals. Record which person or organization owns each decision.

#### 4. A conference conversation should end with a documented next step

Before requesting a private consultation, collect your family structure, residence, embryo or donor status, clinic position, timing priorities, and unanswered questions. The purpose is to make the first planning conversation more specific, not to promise eligibility, matching, pregnancy, birth, or a fixed timeline.

### 建议正文

#### Thinking about surrogacy after MHB? Start with your current facts.

After an event like MHB New York 2026, the next useful step is not to choose a provider from one conversation. Start by recording your current facts: family structure, residence, embryo or donor status, clinic relationship, target timing, budget questions, and the professional decisions that still need independent advice. Then use the same written questions with each provider so you can compare responsibilities, handoffs, communication, and assumptions. Yunda can help organize education, coordination, bilingual communication, and case-management support. A private planning conversation can help identify what to prepare next, but it does not promise eligibility, a match, pregnancy, birth, a fixed cost, or a fixed timeline.

#### A six-part post-event checklist

1. **Family and location:** Who is making decisions, where does the family live, and which jurisdiction questions need a qualified attorney?
2. **Embryos and donor planning:** Are embryos available, is donor support needed, and which clinic or fertility professional owns the medical decision?
3. **Process position:** Which stage is complete, which documents are missing, and what must happen before matching or transfer?
4. **Cost assumptions:** Which amounts are agency coordination, medical, legal, insurance, escrow, travel, donor, contingency, or rematching costs?
5. **Communication:** Who is the case contact, what is the expected response path, and how are multilingual needs handled?
6. **Next conversation:** What three questions must a private planning call answer before any commitment is considered?

#### What Yunda can and cannot do

Yunda Surrogacy provides education, coordination, bilingual communication, and case-management support. Yunda is not a law firm, medical provider, IVF clinic, insurance company, or escrow company. Legal, medical, insurance, IVF, and escrow decisions should be made with the relevant qualified professionals. Individual eligibility, costs, timelines, and outcomes vary by case.
