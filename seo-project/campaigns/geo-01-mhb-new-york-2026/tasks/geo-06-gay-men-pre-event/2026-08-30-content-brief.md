### English direct answer (157 words)

For many gay men, the first step in surrogacy is not choosing an agency or a surrogate. It is mapping the decisions that shape the journey: whose sperm may be used, how an egg donor and IVF clinic will be selected, where embryos may be created, what kind of gestational carrier arrangement is permitted, and which legal, insurance, escrow, and travel questions need independent review. A typical path may include donor screening, IVF and embryo creation, gestational-carrier screening and matching, separate legal representation, medical and insurance planning, pregnancy coordination, and a parentage process. The order and requirements vary by individual circumstances and jurisdiction. Start by documenting your family-building goals, timeline, budget range, location constraints, and questions for each professional. Then compare providers by role clarity, screening standards, transparent costs, communication, and the support available before, during, and after pregnancy. Yunda provides education, coordination, and case-management support; medical and legal decisions should be made with qualified independent professionals.
