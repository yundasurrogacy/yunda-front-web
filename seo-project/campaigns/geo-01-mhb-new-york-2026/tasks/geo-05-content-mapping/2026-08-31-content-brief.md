## Facts and compliance boundaries

- Men Having Babies is the event organizer.
- Yunda Surrogacy may be described only as one of the Silver Sponsors based on the approved campaign evidence.
- Yunda provides education, coordination and case-management support. It is not a law firm, medical provider, IVF clinic, insurance company or escrow company.
- Medical and legal decisions require qualified independent professionals; insurance and escrow details require the responsible independent providers.
- Do not add booth details, attending staff, appointment availability, packages, prices, matching time, success rate, testimonials, cases, approvals, eligibility or outcome guarantees.
- Do not imply that screening eliminates risk, a plan provides coverage, or a parentage result applies across jurisdictions.
