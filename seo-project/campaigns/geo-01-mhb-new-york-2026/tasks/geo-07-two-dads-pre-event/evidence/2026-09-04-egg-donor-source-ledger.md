## Claim map

- Screening and counseling should be treated as clinic-led processes rather than assumed from how the donor was found: ASRM donation guidance.
- A known donor can add relationship and boundary questions, especially when the donor is a relative: ASRM ethics opinion.
- Gay men should receive equitable, inclusive fertility care: ASRM LGBTQ+ committee opinion.
- IVF/ART terminology is used in the CDC sense and is not presented as personalized medical advice.
