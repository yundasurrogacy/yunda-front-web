## Page and intent decision

Do not create a second article URL for the complete two-dads journey. GEO 05 assigns the complete gay/LGBTQ+ family-building path to `/single-parents-lgbtq`; the existing page already owns the relevant audience and includes a matching FAQ. A new page targeting `how does surrogacy work for two dads` would create avoidable overlap with that canonical page and with GEO 06's embedded starting answer.

GEO 07 therefore expands the existing canonical page with a passage-level answer and a five-branch decision map. `/resources/mhb-new-york-2026` remains the unique owner of event facts, while the Two Dads module links to it as a secondary CTA.

## Audience, intent and user task

- Primary audience: two fathers considering U.S. gestational surrogacy.
- Secondary audience: international male couples and single fathers comparing donor, clinic, carrier, legal and insurance handoffs.
- Intent: informational with commercial investigation.
- User task: understand the standard process, identify which branch applies now, and know which qualified professional owns each decision.
- Primary conversion: `Plan My Private Consultation` → `/be-parents`.
- Event conversion: `View the event guide` → `/resources/mhb-new-york-2026`.

## Direct-answer requirements

The answer must:

1. State that a typical two-dads path uses a donor egg, IVF and a gestational carrier.
2. Explain that an IVF clinic owns medical counseling, embryo creation and evaluation.
3. Distinguish the egg donor from the gestational carrier.
4. State the carrier's authority over her own medical care.
5. Require independent, jurisdiction-specific legal review.
6. Treat fertility benefits and carrier maternity coverage as separate insurance questions.
7. Describe Yunda as education, coordination and case-management support only.
8. Avoid success, eligibility, timing, coverage, parentage or cost guarantees.

## Content and compliance boundaries

- Use `gestational carrier` in medical/legal explanations; use respectful audience-friendly language elsewhere.
- Do not imply that a gestational carrier provides the egg.
- Do not state that two fathers will both be legal parents without jurisdiction-specific counsel.
- Do not claim a plan covers donor eggs, IVF, surrogate pregnancy or newborn expenses without plan-level verification.
- Do not publish a real reviewer name or qualification unless that person approves the exact copy.
- Keep Men Having Babies as event organizer and Yunda as Silver Sponsor; do not imply co-organization.
- Do not add booth, attending-team, appointment, price, matching-time, success-rate, case or testimonial claims.
