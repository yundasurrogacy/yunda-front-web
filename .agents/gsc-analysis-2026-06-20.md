## Executive Summary

Yunda has meaningful search visibility but weak non-brand click capture.

Across 92 days, the site generated:

- Clicks: 1,182
- Impressions: 431,056
- Overall CTR: 0.274%
- Weighted average position: 27.4

The site is not struggling because Google never shows it. It is struggling because many high-impression non-brand searches are ranking too low, or ranking in mid positions with snippets that do not earn clicks.

Brand demand is strong:

- Brand queries: 573 clicks, 1,427 impressions, 40.154% CTR
- Non-brand queries: 181 clicks, 259,219 impressions, 0.07% CTR

This means most SEO upside is in non-brand page consolidation, snippet improvement, internal linking, and URL/site-architecture cleanup.

### 2. Non-brand visibility is high but CTR is very weak

Non-brand has 259,219 impressions and only 181 clicks. CTR is 0.07%.

This usually means:

- Pages are ranking too low for competitive commercial terms.
- Multiple similar pages split topical authority.
- Titles/meta descriptions may not match search intent.
- Blog posts are attracting broad impressions but not the right click intent.
- Business pages are not clearly established as the canonical answers for money and surrogate recruitment queries.

## Site Architecture Implications

The previous site architecture finding is validated by GSC:

- `/resources` and `Surrogate Resources` conflict semantically.
- Surrogate candidate pages are scattered across root-level URLs.
- Intended-parent cost/process pages and surrogate compensation/process pages are too close in naming.
- Blog posts are collecting impressions for commercial queries that should be owned by durable service pages.

Recommendation:

- Keep `/resources` as `Resources & Media Center`.
- Rename `Surrogate Resources` to `For Surrogates` or `Surrogate Guide`.
- Create a structured surrogate hub and intended-parent hub.
- Use 301 redirects only after a migration plan is finalized.
