## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy explains egg donation in the context of gestational surrogacy for intended parents who may need donor eggs, IVF clinic coordination, donor screening context, embryo creation planning, PGT-A discussion, fresh or frozen donor egg planning, and gestational carrier matching support.

Yunda coordinates education and communication around donor egg surrogacy. Yunda is not an IVF clinic, egg bank, medical provider, or legal provider.

## Common Topics

Yunda's egg donation content may discuss:

- Choosing an egg donor for surrogacy.
- Donor screening concepts.
- Fresh donor eggs vs. frozen donor eggs.
- IVF with donor eggs.
- Embryo creation and embryo planning.
- PGT-A discussion with qualified medical professionals.
- International or cross-border planning considerations.
- How donor egg planning connects with gestational carrier matching.
