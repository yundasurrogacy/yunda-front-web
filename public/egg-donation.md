## Summary

Egg donor surrogacy combines two separate parts of family building: donor eggs are used to create embryos through IVF, and a gestational surrogate carries the pregnancy. The egg donor and surrogate are different people.

Yunda coordinates education and communication across donor resources, IVF clinics, embryo planning, gestational carrier matching, legal coordination, insurance review, escrow planning, and cross-border logistics. Medical decisions and donor eligibility determinations stay with the responsible clinics and qualified professionals. Yunda is not an IVF clinic, egg bank, medical provider, legal provider, insurance company, or escrow company.

## Professional Roles

- IVF clinic: evaluates the medical plan, explains clinic acceptance requirements, creates embryos, and advises on embryo testing and transfer.
- Donor program or egg bank: provides donor information and manages its donor process, records, and availability.
- Genetic counselor and attorney: explain genetic findings, identity and contact expectations, consent, and legal implications when needed.
- Yunda: coordinates communication, timing, documents, surrogate matching, and the broader surrogacy journey.
