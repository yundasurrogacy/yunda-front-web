## Legal coordination

California surrogacy planning may include:

- Separate legal counsel for intended parents and the surrogate.
- Drafting and review of the surrogacy agreement.
- Legal clearance communication before embryo transfer.
- Parentage planning, including questions about a pre-birth order where applicable.
- Hospital, birth-record, and post-birth document coordination.

Yunda Surrogacy may coordinate introductions, information flow, schedules, and case milestones. A qualified attorney must explain the agreement, rights, responsibilities, jurisdiction, parentage process, and any pre-birth order requirements. Yunda Surrogacy does not provide legal advice or determine legal eligibility or outcomes.

## ART and IVF coordination

ART means assisted reproductive technology. In a gestational surrogacy journey, the IVF clinic and medical professionals determine medical eligibility, testing, treatment protocol, medication, embryo creation or handling, embryo transfer, pregnancy care, and clinical recommendations.

Yunda Surrogacy may help organize records, clinic questions, appointments, travel, communication, legal-clearance handoffs, and non-medical readiness. Yunda Surrogacy is not an IVF clinic or medical provider and does not make ART treatment decisions.

Published ART or IVF statistics describe the reporting population and method used by the source. They do not predict an individual family’s outcome. This summary does not state a clinic success rate, acceptance rate, pregnancy rate, birth rate, or other guaranteed result.

## Insurance and ART insurance planning

Insurance review may address:

- The surrogate’s current maternity coverage and eligibility.
- Policy exclusions, including possible surrogacy or assisted-reproduction exclusions.
- Waiting periods, deductibles, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket maximums.
- Pregnancy, hospital, delivery, and newborn coverage timing.
- Supplemental insurance or ART-related coverage needs.
- Claims, documentation, billing, and coordination questions.

The **$12,600 insurance review and premium budget** is a 2026 planning estimate associated with the **$142,750 estimated program total**. It does not establish that a specific policy will be available, accepted, issued, or sufficient. An insurance professional or carrier must confirm current coverage, exclusions, premium, underwriting, claims handling, and any required supplemental policy.

The California Department of Managed Health Care’s revised October 5, 2026 APL 25-021 addresses infertility and fertility services for eligible full-service commercial plans, including certain medical screening and fertility services connected with surrogate use. The APL states that the enrollee’s health plan is not responsible for the surrogate’s health care costs after embryo transfer, including maternity services, except as required by the surrogate’s own health plan contract. Plan type, contract dates, medical necessity, and the specific policy still control. [DMHC APL 25-021](https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/Portals/0/Docs/OPL/APL25-021ImplementationofSenateBill729%282024%29-REVISED%2810_5_2026%29.pdf)

Yunda Surrogacy can coordinate communication and help track questions and documents. Yunda Surrogacy does not sell insurance, underwrite risk, issue a policy, decide claims, or guarantee coverage.

## Escrow and trust-account planning

An independent escrow or trust-account provider may:

- Hold funds under its account terms and applicable instructions.
- Release funds according to the written agreement and documented milestones.
- Record compensation, approved reimbursements, insurance-related amounts, and other authorized expenses.
- Provide statements, receipts, or reconciliation information according to its process.

Yunda Surrogacy may coordinate the funding timeline, communication, and document handoffs. Yunda Surrogacy does not hold client funds, administer an escrow account, decide independent releases, or guarantee a payment outcome. The provider’s terms and the written agreement control.
