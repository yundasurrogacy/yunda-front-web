## Summary

Surrogacy cost in California and the United States varies by agency coordination, surrogate compensation, IVF clinic fees, legal work, insurance, escrow administration, medical events, pregnancy details, and case-specific factors.

Yunda's cost content is intended to help intended parents understand categories and planning questions. It is not a guaranteed quote.

## Common Cost Categories

Surrogacy budgets may include:

- Surrogacy agency coordination.
- Surrogate compensation and allowances.
- Medical and psychological screening coordination.
- Legal agreement coordination.
- Parentage or court-related legal steps.
- Insurance review and insurance-related planning.
- Escrow or trust account administration.
- Travel, childcare, lost wages, or pregnancy-related reimbursements when approved.
- Case-dependent items such as multiples, C-section, bed rest, or complications.

## Usually Separate From Agency Cost Estimates

Depending on the program scope, these may be separate:

- IVF clinic fees.
- Egg retrieval, donor egg, embryo creation, PGT, storage, and transfer-related clinic billing.
- Newborn medical care after birth.
- NICU or pediatric costs.
- Additional legal, medical, or insurance items not included in the written agreement.

## Why Cost Varies

Cost varies because each journey can differ by:

- State and legal process.
- IVF clinic pathway.
- Embryo status.
- Insurance eligibility, exclusions, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums.
- Surrogate compensation structure.
- Medical events during pregnancy.
- Singleton vs. twins.
- Travel and logistics.
- Agreement terms and approved reimbursements.
