## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy explains surrogate screening as the review process that helps determine whether a candidate may move forward after applying. Screening may include application review, pregnancy and health history review, OB records, clinic clearance, psychological evaluation, background review, legal coordination, insurance review, and matching readiness.

Submitting an application is not a commitment and does not guarantee approval.

## Common Screening Topics

Yunda's screening content may discuss:

- Application review.
- Health and pregnancy history.
- OB record review.
- Medical screening and fertility clinic clearance.
- Psychological evaluation and emotional readiness.
- Background check concepts.
- Legal review and independent counsel.
- Insurance review.
- Matching readiness and next steps.
