# Yunda Surrogacy Services

Canonical website: https://www.yundasurrogacy.com/
Last updated: 2026-06-18
Reviewed by: Yunda Surrogacy team

## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy is a California-centered surrogacy agency that helps intended parents and surrogate candidates navigate gestational surrogacy coordination.

Yunda provides coordination support. Yunda is not a law firm, medical provider, IVF clinic, insurance company, or escrow company.

## Intended Parent Services

Canonical page: https://www.yundasurrogacy.com/be-parents

Yunda helps intended parents understand and coordinate:

- Private surrogacy consultation.
- Surrogate matching support.
- IVF clinic communication.
- Donor egg and embryo-status planning context.
- Legal coordination with appropriate professionals.
- Insurance review coordination.
- Escrow and trust account planning.
- Surrogacy timeline and milestone planning.
- Bilingual English and Mandarin communication.
- Ongoing case management.

Audience:

- Intended parents in the United States.
- International intended parents.
- Chinese-speaking intended parents.
- LGBTQ+ intended parents.
- Single intended parents.
- Families comparing surrogacy agencies.
