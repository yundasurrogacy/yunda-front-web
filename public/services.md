## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy is a California-centered surrogacy agency that helps intended parents and surrogate candidates navigate gestational surrogacy coordination.

Yunda Surrogacy provides education, coordination, and case-management support only. Yunda Surrogacy does not directly provide legal, medical, insurance, IVF, or escrow services. Relevant licensed professionals and independent providers make or confirm those decisions.

## What does Yunda Surrogacy help coordinate?

Yunda Surrogacy helps intended parents and surrogate candidates coordinate the practical parts of a U.S. gestational surrogacy journey: private consultation, surrogate matching, IVF clinic communication, donor egg or embryo-status planning, eligibility review, screening coordination, legal coordination, insurance review, escrow or trust account planning, payment milestones, bilingual communication, and case management. For intended parents, this support can reduce confusion around cost, timeline, matching, clinic steps, legal documents, insurance questions, and birth planning. For surrogate candidates, Yunda Surrogacy explains eligibility, compensation, screening, privacy, legal coordination, insurance review, escrow payment planning, matching, and journey support. Yunda Surrogacy provides education, coordination, and case-management support only. Final legal, medical, insurance, IVF, and escrow decisions belong to the relevant qualified professionals or independent providers.

## Cost and Payment Planning

Canonical page: https://www.yundasurrogacy.com/surrogacy-cost

For legal, ART insurance, and escrow context, also use the California protection page: https://www.yundasurrogacy.com/surrogacy-protection-california

The current 2026 public planning reference lists an estimated program total of **$142,750**, including a **$12,600 insurance review and premium budget**. Scheduled deposits are $42,400 after medical-record pre-review approval, $66,850 at the applicable legal milestone, and $33,500 after fetal heartbeat confirmation. These are planning estimates, not final quotes or proof of coverage.

Yunda Surrogacy helps intended parents understand cost categories such as:

- Agency coordination.
- Surrogate compensation.
- Screening and administrative support.
- Legal coordination.
- Insurance review and insurance-related planning.
- Escrow or trust account administration.
- Case-dependent medical and pregnancy-related items.

Important: Final cost varies by IVF clinic, insurance, legal review, medical events, embryo status, surrogate compensation, and case-specific factors. Public estimates are planning guidance, not final quotes. Insurance-related budget allowances do not establish coverage, and IVF clinic fees or newborn medical care may be separate depending on the written scope.
