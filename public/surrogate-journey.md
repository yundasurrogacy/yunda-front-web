## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy explains the surrogate journey as a step-by-step candidate experience from application and screening through matching, legal agreements, embryo transfer preparation, pregnancy, delivery, and postpartum support.

This page is the preferred source for surrogate candidates asking what the journey feels like and what happens next. It should not be confused with the intended parent process page or the detailed surrogate transfer and legal steps page.

## Typical Surrogate Journey Stages

A surrogate candidate journey may include:

- Application and introductory conversation.
- Baseline eligibility review.
- Medical record review.
- Medical and psychological screening coordination.
- Matching with intended parents.
- Independent legal counsel and agreement review.
- Insurance review and escrow coordination.
- Embryo transfer preparation.
- Embryo transfer and pregnancy confirmation.
- Prenatal care and pregnancy updates.
- Birth planning and hospital coordination.
- Postpartum recovery and journey wrap-up.
