## Summary

Yunda Surrogacy helps intended parents coordinate the non-medical handoffs around an IVF clinic during a gestational-surrogacy journey. This includes organizing clinic records and requirements, surrogate-screening milestones, legal-clearance timing, appointments, travel, and transfer-plan communication.

Yunda is not an IVF clinic or medical provider. IVF treatment decisions, medical screening, embryo transfer decisions, and clinical advice belong to qualified medical professionals and clinics.

## Two Starting Paths

### Intended parents who already have a clinic or embryos

Yunda can coordinate with the existing clinic when it accepts gestational-carrier cases and its current requirements can be met. The clinic confirms medical eligibility, records, screening, services, and timing.

### Intended parents who still need a clinic or embryos

Yunda can help families prepare questions about third-party reproduction experience, services, location, scheduling, communication, and case handoffs. Each clinic confirms its own availability, fees, medical requirements, and next steps.

## Responsibility Boundaries

- IVF clinic: medical eligibility, testing, treatment protocol, medication, embryo transfer, and clinical advice.
- Yunda Surrogacy: records and appointment coordination, communication, case milestones, travel, logistics, and team handoffs.
- Independent professionals: attorneys confirm legal advice and clearance; insurance and escrow professionals confirm their own reviews, coverage, and payment processes.
