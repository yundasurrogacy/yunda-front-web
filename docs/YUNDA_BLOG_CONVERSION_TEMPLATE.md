# Yunda Blog Conversion Template

Date: 2026-07-05

Purpose: define a Yunda blog page structure that answers customer questions clearly and moves qualified readers toward consultation, without making the article feel like a hard-sell landing page.

Based on:

- `docs/COMPETITOR_BLOG_LAYOUT_STRUCTURE_ANALYSIS.md`
- Physician's Surrogacy layout pattern: clean article + right TOC/CTA rail
- Surrogacy4All layout pattern: summary, key takeaways, tables, author/trust, related posts

## Core Strategy

Yunda blog pages should work like guided consultation content:

1. Answer the reader's immediate question quickly.
2. Help them understand risks, options, process, cost, timeline, and next steps.
3. Build trust through medical/legal/process clarity.
4. Offer a consultation at natural decision points.

The blog should not only be an SEO article. It should be a soft conversion page for people researching surrogacy, egg donation, insurance, cost, legal process, medical screening, or agency comparison.

## Recommended Page Structure

```text
Header
Breadcrumb
Featured Image
Article Meta
H1
Short Intro

Main Article Grid
  Left Column
    Quick Answer
    Key Takeaways
    Main Educational Sections
    Tables / Checklists / Timelines
    FAQ
    Final CTA
    Author / Reviewed By
    Related Posts

  Right Rail
    Table of Contents
    Consultation CTA
    Related Guides
    Trust Mini Card

Footer
```

## Desktop Layout

### 1. Header

Use the existing Yunda header, but keep it quiet on blog pages.

Goal:

- Let users navigate if needed.
- Avoid distracting from the article.

Avoid:

- Heavy dropdowns.
- Extra utility bars.
- Too many competing CTAs in the nav.

### 2. Breadcrumb

Format:

```text
Home > Blog > Current Article
```

Goal:

- Help users understand where they are.
- Support SEO/internal linking.
- Make the page feel organized and trustworthy.

### 3. Featured Image

Place a strong image near the top before or directly around the title area.

Goal:

- Give the article an immediate human/service context.
- Make the page feel more credible and less like a plain CMS post.

Best use:

- Medical consultation.
- Surrogate/intended parent journey.
- Insurance/legal/process imagery.
- Realistic service-related imagery, not decorative abstract graphics.

### 4. Article Meta

Show compact chips:

- Published or updated date.
- Category.
- Reading time.
- Optional audience tag: Intended Parents, Surrogates, International Families.

Goal:

- Set expectations.
- Show freshness.
- Help the reader know whether the article is for them.

### 5. H1 + Short Intro

The H1 should match the user's search question. The intro should confirm the article will answer it directly.

Goal:

- Capture search intent.
- Reduce bounce by telling the reader they are in the right place.

Example structure:

```text
H1: Surrogate Mother Health Insurance Questions for 2026
Intro: If you are planning a surrogacy journey, insurance is usually one of the first areas that feels unclear. This guide explains what to check, what can go wrong, and when to speak with an agency before moving forward.
```

## Main Article Grid

Desktop should use a two-column layout:

- Left content column: around 680-760px readable width.
- Right rail: around 280-340px.

The left column solves the question. The right rail guides the next action.

## Left Column Modules

### 1. Quick Answer

Place this immediately after the intro.

Purpose:

- Give impatient readers a direct answer.
- Improve AI/SEO answer extraction.
- Reduce friction for users who do not want to read a full article first.

Recommended format:

```text
Quick Answer
2-4 short paragraphs or bullets that answer the core question directly.
```

Customer question solved:

- "Can you just tell me the answer first?"

Conversion role:

- Builds trust fast.
- Encourages serious readers to continue.

### 2. Key Takeaways

Place this after Quick Answer.

Purpose:

- Summarize the most important points.
- Help scanning users understand the article.

Recommended format:

- 4-6 bullets.
- Each bullet should be specific, not generic.

Customer question solved:

- "What are the main things I need to know?"

Conversion role:

- Makes Yunda feel organized and expert.
- Creates a natural point for a light CTA after the box.

### 3. Main Educational Sections

Use clear H2 sections that match the reader's decision process.

Recommended H2 patterns:

- What this means
- Why it matters
- What to check before you decide
- Common risks
- What happens next
- When to talk to an agency

Customer questions solved:

- "What does this mean for my situation?"
- "What should I be careful about?"
- "What is the process?"
- "How much time or money could this affect?"

Conversion role:

- Turns search traffic into informed leads.
- Helps readers realize when professional guidance is needed.

### 4. Tables

Use tables when comparing options, risks, timelines, costs, or responsibilities.

Good table types:

- Agency vs self-managed process.
- Covered vs not covered by insurance.
- Before match vs after match screening.
- State-by-state legal difference.
- Intended parent vs surrogate responsibilities.

Customer question solved:

- "What are my options, and how do they compare?"

Conversion role:

- Makes complex topics easier to understand.
- Supports trust because Yunda is not hiding complexity.

### 5. Checklist / Timeline / Process Block

Use when the topic has steps.

Recommended formats:

- Checklist: "Before you move forward, confirm these items."
- Timeline: "What happens in the next 1-8 weeks."
- Process: "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3."

Customer question solved:

- "What should I do next?"
- "Where am I in the journey?"

Conversion role:

- Makes the consultation CTA feel useful, not random.

### 6. Inline CTA

Place one inline CTA after the reader has received useful information, usually after the first 30-40% of the article.

Recommended CTA copy:

```text
Not sure how this applies to your situation?
Talk with Yunda's team before you make the next decision.
Button: Schedule a Consultation
```

Customer question solved:

- "My case is more specific. Who can I ask?"

Conversion role:

- Captures high-intent users without interrupting early reading.

### 7. FAQ

Place near the end.

Purpose:

- Handle final objections.
- Support SEO and future FAQ schema.

Good FAQ questions:

- How do I know if this applies to me?
- What documents should I prepare?
- When should I talk to an agency?
- What happens if something changes later?
- Can international intended parents use this process?

Customer question solved:

- "I still have a few practical questions."

Conversion role:

- Reduces hesitation before consultation.

### 8. Final CTA

Place after FAQ.

Recommended structure:

```text
Ready to discuss your surrogacy plan?
Yunda can help you understand the process, risks, timeline, and next steps.
Button: Book a Free Consultation
Secondary: Contact Us
```

Customer question solved:

- "What should I do now?"

Conversion role:

- Converts readers who reached the end and have enough trust to act.

### 9. Author / Reviewed By

Place below the final CTA or just before related posts.

Recommended fields:

- Author name.
- Role.
- Short bio.
- Medical/legal/reviewer note if applicable.
- Last reviewed/updated date.

Customer question solved:

- "Who is giving me this information?"

Conversion role:

- Adds trust, especially for medical, legal, insurance, and cost topics.

### 10. Related Posts

Place after author/reviewer.

Recommended:

- 3-4 related posts.
- Match by user journey stage, not just category.

Examples:

- Insurance article links to cost, legal, surrogate requirements, consultation page.
- Screening article links to matching process, medical requirements, surrogate compensation.

Customer question solved:

- "What else should I read before deciding?"

Conversion role:

- Keeps readers inside Yunda's ecosystem.

## Right Rail Modules

### 1. Table of Contents

Generate from H2/H3 headings.

Behavior:

- Sticky on desktop.
- Collapsible or hidden on mobile.
- Highlight current section if possible.

Purpose:

- Help long-form readers navigate.
- Show the page is comprehensive.

### 2. Consultation CTA Card

Keep this compact.

Recommended content:

```text
Have questions about your case?
Speak with Yunda's surrogacy team.
Button: Schedule Consultation
```

Avoid:

- A long form in the sidebar.
- Too many fields before the reader trusts the page.

Purpose:

- Always provide the next step.
- Keep conversion available without interrupting reading.

### 3. Related Guides

Show 3-5 relevant internal links.

Examples:

- Surrogacy Cost
- Surrogacy Process
- Surrogate Requirements
- Insurance Coverage
- Legal Protection

Purpose:

- Help users self-educate.
- Improve internal linking.

### 4. Trust Mini Card

Short trust signal.

Possible content:

- Bilingual support.
- California/US process guidance.
- Intended parent and surrogate support.
- Medical/legal coordination.

Purpose:

- Answer "why Yunda?" without turning the blog into a sales page.

## Mobile Layout

Mobile should become single-column:

1. Header.
2. Breadcrumb.
3. Featured image.
4. Meta + H1.
5. Intro.
6. Quick Answer.
7. Key Takeaways.
8. Collapsible Table of Contents.
9. Article body.
10. Inline CTA.
11. FAQ.
12. Final CTA.
13. Author/reviewer.
14. Related posts.

Important:

- Do not show a long sidebar form near the top on mobile.
- Put CTA after value has been delivered.
- Keep tables horizontally scrollable.

## Conversion Logic

| Page Area | User Need | Conversion Role |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Answer | Get the direct answer | Earn trust quickly |
| Key Takeaways | Understand main points fast | Keep scanners engaged |
| TOC | Navigate long content | Increase reading depth |
| Tables | Compare options clearly | Reduce confusion |
| Checklist/Timeline | Know next steps | Create readiness for consultation |
| Inline CTA | Ask about personal situation | Capture mid-article intent |
| FAQ | Remove objections | Prepare reader to act |
| Final CTA | Decide what to do next | Convert high-trust readers |
| Author/Reviewed By | Verify credibility | Increase confidence |
| Related Posts | Continue research | Keep user in Yunda site |

## Recommended Backend/Content Fields Later

To support this template well, the blog editor can eventually add:

- `quick_answer`
- `key_takeaways`
- `reviewed_by`
- `reviewed_at`
- `reading_time`
- `cta_variant`
- `related_post_ids`

Current rich text can still support most of the layout if the frontend generates TOC from headings and styles tables, lists, FAQ, and CTA blocks consistently.
