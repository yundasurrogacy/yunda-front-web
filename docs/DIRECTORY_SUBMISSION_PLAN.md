# Yunda Directory Submission Plan

Last updated: 2026-06-19

## Bottom Line

Yunda Surrogacy should not be submitted as a generic SaaS, AI tool, MCP agent, or no-code product. The strongest directory and backlink path is:

1. Fertility, surrogacy, LGBTQ+ family-building, and intended-parent provider directories.
2. Local business and review profiles for California / Los Angeles service discovery.
3. High-authority profile and content platforms that can link to Yunda's authoritative service pages.
4. Editorial outreach to "best surrogacy agency" and fertility resource articles.

AI tool directories should be skipped unless Yunda launches a real AI tool, calculator, chatbot, or intake assistant with its own public product page.

## Review Request Plan

Goal: 10 public reviews in 30 days.

1. Identify 20 satisfied intended parents, surrogate candidates, professional partners, or appropriate reviewers who can speak truthfully about communication, education, transparency, and coordination.
2. Send a personal review request with direct Google review link.
3. Ask for honest feedback; do not script the review.
4. Follow up once after 5-7 days.
5. After reviews are live, cite Google review links only without fabricating star ratings or counts.
